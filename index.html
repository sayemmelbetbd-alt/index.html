Protect your account

Security & Privacy

Protect your account details while registering, requesting a promo code or discussing payment questions.

  • Never disclose passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or security answers.
  • Use a unique password and secure the email account linked to your affiliate profile.
  • Check the destination and spelling of links before signing in.
  • Do not send identity documents or payment details through unofficial channels unless the platform explicitly requires them through a secure official process.
  • Use only promotional material and practices permitted by the affiliate program and local law.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I register as an affiliate?

Use the affiliate registration link provided on this website. Complete the form on the platform and follow its verification instructions.

How do I request a custom promo code?

Register first, contact SAYEM through Telegram, provide the email used during registration, and suggest two or three preferred code options. Assignment depends on availability and approval.

Is a custom promo code guaranteed?

No. Your preferred option may be assigned only if it is available and approved. The program or manager may request additional steps.

When are commission updates and payments?

The owner-supplied information on this page says Monday for commission updates and Tuesday for the payment schedule. Confirm the current details and eligibility in your affiliate account.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The owner-supplied threshold is $30. It is expected to appear under “Available Withdraw” once the eligible balance reaches that amount, but actual availability depends on current program rules.

Do I need to link a player account?

According to the owner's current information, the required player account must be added or linked before payment. Follow the instructions currently displayed in your affiliate account.

Can I send my password or OTP to the manager?

No. Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or sensitive login credentials. A registration email and preferred promo code options are sufficient for the described code request.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Affiliate earnings are not guaranteed and depend on program terms, activity, eligibility and other factors. Review the official rules before participating.
