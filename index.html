Eligibility and responsible participation

Read the rules before taking part.

Eligibility and local rules

Participation is subject to the affiliate program's eligibility requirements, its current terms, and applicable laws in your location. You are responsible for checking whether participation and promotion are lawful where you live.

  • Provide accurate registration information.
  • Review program policies before promoting any service.
  • Do not target people who are under the legal age for the relevant activity.

Responsible participation

Where the service relates to betting or gambling, it carries financial risk and is not a reliable way to earn money. Never promise results or encourage people to risk money they cannot afford to lose. Stop and seek appropriate support if participation becomes difficult to control.

  • No earnings or approval are guaranteed.
  • Do not mislead users with unsupported claims.
  • Respect platform rules, advertising standards, and local restrictions.
